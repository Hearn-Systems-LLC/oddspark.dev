# Generation Modes

Both modes share one button and the optional domain field; the presence of a domain selects the mode. Both return a confident, low-friction plan Hearn Systems could credibly implement.

## Website-grounded mode (domain supplied)

Treat the public business website as an identity key and evidence source. Inspect:

- Stated capabilities and missing capabilities
- CTAs, forms, links, contact channels
- Integrations and navigation complexity
- Technical behavior, broken images or links, stale content

Combine these signals with general vertical knowledge. Surface only one charming breadcrumb showing why the spark belongs to this business; do not present an audit.

## Coherent local mode (no domain)

Combine:

- Fixed Port Huron / Blue Water Area small-business context
- Current date/time and seasonality
- A compatible business situation
- A Hearn-deliverable capability bundle

Seed around software, AI, automation, integrations, data workflows, online opportunities, and adjacent digital systems. Do **not** select independent random axes and rationalize their collision afterward.

## Current → target behavior

- **Current failure mode:** independent random axes collide into contrived ideas, duplicate existing capabilities, assume the wrong channel, mismatch solution scale, criticize without a constructive intervention, or recommend work outside Hearn Systems' delivery envelope.
- **Target behavior:** recognize a familiar habitual process, use compatible evidence and context to uncover a longstanding annoyance, propose one surprising but imaginable improvement, and explain how the change protects the owner's existing tools, authority, and routines.

## Evidence and privacy boundary

- By default, use only what the submitted public business website reveals, plus general vertical knowledge and current date/time.
- No personally identifiable information, cookies, sessions, or other user data.
- No Google reviews or broader business-footprint investigation.
- No additional input field; the domain remains the only optional input.
- Numeric claims only when grounded in real numbers on the public site; otherwise qualitative, avoiding fabricated ROI or false precision.
