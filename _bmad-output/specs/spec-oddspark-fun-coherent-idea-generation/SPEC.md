# Oddspark Coherence-Gated Opportunity Brief Pipeline

## Why

A pain to solve and an opportunity to capture. Oddspark's current independent random-axis generation collides axes into contrived ideas: it can duplicate capabilities a business already has, assume the wrong channel, mismatch solution scale, criticize without a constructive intervention, or recommend work outside Hearn Systems' delivery envelope. The opportunity is to replace that with a coherence-gated Opportunity Brief pipeline that recognizes a familiar habitual process, uncovers a longstanding annoyance from compatible evidence, proposes one surprising-but-imaginable improvement, and shows how the change protects the owner's existing tools, authority, and routines — turning each spark into a credible, low-friction entry point for a Hearn Systems feasibility conversation. Governing principle: do not break what already works; preserve every helpful part of the existing process; find the smallest worthwhile change around a trusted routine.

## Capabilities

- **CAP-1**
  - **intent:** With no domain supplied, generate a coherent, seasonally relevant spark grounded in Port Huron / Blue Water Area small-business context, current date/time, a compatible business situation, and a Hearn-deliverable capability bundle (software, AI, automation, integrations, data workflows, online opportunities, adjacent digital systems) — never independent random axes rationalized after the fact.
  - **success:** A no-domain run produces a spark that passes all 9 coherence gates in `coherence-gates.md`.

- **CAP-2**
  - **intent:** With a domain supplied, treat the public business website as identity key and evidence source — stated and missing capabilities, CTAs, forms, links, contact channels, integrations, navigation complexity, technical behavior, broken images/links, stale content — combined with general vertical knowledge, to generate a website-grounded spark.
  - **success:** A domain run produces a spark whose Why-It-Fits contains exactly one restrained, charming website breadcrumb (not an audit) and passes all 9 coherence gates.

- **CAP-3**
  - **intent:** Evaluate every candidate against the 9 coherence gates and reject it if any gate fails.
  - **success:** The representative contradiction set — existing scheduler, channel mismatch, undersized/oversized solution, unactionable criticism, non-digital advice — is rejected; only gate-passing candidates render.

- **CAP-4**
  - **intent:** Render every result as a confident plan following the 8-element result-card contract, including a preliminary change estimate (time range plus workflow-step impact) and an explicit what-stays-the-same statement, ending in a spark-specific no-pressure implementation invitation.
  - **success:** Every displayed result contains all 8 elements in `result-card-contract.md` and names preserved tools, decision authority, and untouched workflow steps.

## Constraints

- The interface keeps exactly one button and one optional domain field; no new inputs, no increased interaction complexity.
- Evidence is limited to the submitted public business website, general vertical knowledge, and current date/time. No PII, cookies, sessions, Google reviews, or broader business-footprint research.
- Numeric claims appear only when grounded in real numbers on the public site; otherwise effects are qualitative — no fabricated ROI or false precision.
- No pricing before discovery. The CTA is problem-solving-led ("talk through the problem, no pitch attached"), no-pressure, no-obligation, and open to concluding the idea is not worth changing.
- Never recommend duplicating a detected site capability or casually replacing a helpful existing process; existing tools are constraints or extension points, not duplication targets.
- Every recommendation must lie within Hearn Systems' credible delivery envelope: custom software, AI automation, integrations, data workflows, or adjacent digital systems.
- Research stays backstage; at most one business-specific breadcrumb appears in the result.

## Non-goals

- Adding user-input fields or increasing interaction complexity.
- Conducting a website audit or surfacing faults without solutions.
- Using reviews, off-site footprint research, personal data, or behavioral surveillance.
- Generic sales copy, unsupported ROI, false precision, or pre-discovery pricing.
- Recommending unrelated operational purchases or work Hearn Systems could not credibly deliver.
- Replacing functioning tools or processes merely to make an idea appear transformative.

## Success signal

Pressing the single button — with or without a domain — produces a result a visitor could retell naturally to a friend: a recognizable routine, a fitting Hearn-deliverable capability, a qualitative payoff, an honest statement of what stays the same, and an invitation they can act on without pressure. Every displayed result passes all 9 gates and the full result-card contract.
