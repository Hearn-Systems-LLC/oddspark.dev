# Risks and Unknowns

- Website evidence may be incomplete, stale, contradictory, or insufficient to infer actual operations.
- Observable scale and maintenance capacity are estimates; proportionality judgments may be uncertain.
- Current website capabilities can be present but poorly exposed, creating duplication risk.
- Time ranges and workflow-step counts are necessarily preliminary until discovery.
- The threshold between a pleasingly surprising idea and an implausible one needs evaluation.
- The evidence needed to consistently avoid obvious ideas while remaining website-only is not yet proven.
