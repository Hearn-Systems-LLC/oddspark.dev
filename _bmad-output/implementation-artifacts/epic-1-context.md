# Epic 1 Context: A Coherent Brief From One Button — local mode live in production

## Goal

A press without a domain produces a committed, gate-passed local Brief or an approved house Brief in production. The result is grounded in Port Huron / Blue Water Area context, passes independently qualified generation and judging, and is enabled through one atomic local-only activation. This epic establishes the safe foundation for later domain grounding, production proof, contact handoff, and legacy retirement.

## Requirements & Constraints

- Preserve the existing one-button interaction, optional domain field, shell, router, seed feeds, security guards, and cache conventions except at explicitly governed seams.
- Local Evidence uses approved regional, seasonal, date, situation, and delivery-capability priors. It is deterministic, frozen, consumes no model call, and fails as a typed error rather than returning partial evidence.
- Every rendered candidate must pass closed schema, grounding, privacy, personal-name, and numeric-claim checks before one candidate-bound semantic judgment across all nine coherence gates, tone, and claims. Malformed, ambiguous, repaired, unbound, unknown, or unqualified outputs fail closed.
- A strike has one route-entry deadline and a six-model-call ledger. Only complete generation-and-judge pairs may start; local mode permits at most three candidates. Invoked failures and timeouts consume capacity. Exhaustion, unsafe output, or insufficient time resolves to an approved, gate-passing house Brief.
- Every Brief contains the eight required result elements in their fixed order and uses confident, plain, non-sales language. Local Briefs contain no business-specific assertions, numeric outcomes, ROI claims, or pricing. The invitation refers to the specific idea and explicitly permits deciding it is not worth changing.
- Owner-approved, hash-bound voice guidance, golden and anti-golden examples, seasonal priors, and house Briefs are production prerequisites. Placeholder content cannot activate.
- Offline fixtures and CI must have no callable production provider or binding and may not deploy or mutate remote resources. Every live or metered run requires fresh approval for its exact frozen configuration, call cap, maximum cost, and retained fields.
- Local committed artifacts expire exactly 30 days after commit without sliding on reads. Coordinator uncertainty returns a negotiated 502, renders no artifact, and increments no served metric.
- Deployment, activation, production proof, receipt-claim activation, and later public promotion are separate authorities. A valid NO-GO remains immutable evidence and blocks only its dependents; it cannot be converted into a pass by rerunning or weakening thresholds.

## Technical Decisions

- Use a pipes-and-filters pipeline with immutable, closed, versioned handoffs: Evidence, Generate, Local Gate, semantic Judge, authoritative Commit, then Render. The strike orchestrator alone owns retries, role selection, deadline accounting, and the call ledger.
- Candidate and evidence references are deterministic hashes of canonical validated values. The judge must echo the exact candidate reference, and adapters may only remove validated wrapper metadata or map a verdict losslessly.
- Generation and judging qualify independently, even when they share a provider or model. Each identity freezes provider, resolved model, parameters, prompts, wire schema, adapter, runtime, binding, and timeout policy. Primary and fallback results never pool. Structural, semantic, and full-request evidence are separate gates.
- Pin and review the Worker toolchain, compatibility date, generated bindings, and runtime configuration before live evidence. Any runtime-bound change invalidates affected structural evidence and every dependent qualification or release decision.
- The typed Brief is the only input to HTML, text, JSON, and sharing renderers; raw model text and legacy personalization state never reach presentation.
- The coordinator is authoritative for claim, read, commit, concurrency resolution, and aggregate metrics. KV values are best-effort projections. Missing or stale projections consult the coordinator before generation, so concurrent requests converge on one immutable committed receipt.
- Roll out reader first, then a compatible inactive writer, then replace the entire activation manifest atomically. The local-only manifest enables local mode, leaves domain mode and later claim/handoff references disabled, and is rollbackable independently from code deployment.
- Representation negotiation is deterministic: explicit JSON preference wins; browser-form or HTML-capable requests receive shell HTML. Dynamic responses use correct content types, vary by origin/accept/content type, and are never cache authority.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

Keep the existing playful shell and input simplicity while making the result a calm, retellable plan. Render all eight Brief elements consistently across enhanced and full-document paths, preserve keyboard and screen-reader semantics, and provide accessible loading, terminal-state, diagram, and fallback text. Enhanced results move focus appropriately; fresh full-document responses do not script focus. During this epic the invitation remains a plain same-tab Hearn contact link, and receipt copy must not claim same-window proof before that claim is separately activated.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

The safety baseline and frozen runtime precede qualification. Approved voice, priors, contracts, and house content feed evidence, generation, gating, orchestration, and rendering. Structural generation and judge qualification precede semantic and full-request qualification. Authoritative commit and compatibility reading precede production rendering and retention. The release sequence is readiness view, compatibility-reader deployment, inactive-writer deployment, then separately approved atomic local-only activation. Until domain activation, a submitted domain follows the governed local path with an honest downgrade notice and never reaches the legacy generator.
