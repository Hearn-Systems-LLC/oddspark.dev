## Integrity limitations

The original result is not audit-grade provider evidence:

1. Its verifier does not close and type-check every nested result object or recompute authorization/headroom consistency. Unknown or altered nested metadata can pass verification.
2. The runner checks a generic loopback health response but does not bind retained evidence to the exact running adapter/config fingerprint. The artifact therefore does not independently prove the provider origin of the retained answers.
3. `fixture_results: 18/18` counts test functions, not the 62 individual contract and normalization cases, and the original Markdown omits the detailed fixture, latency, usage, and failure-taxonomy observations above.

These limitations cannot improve any output into a valid verdict or turn the conservative NO-GO into GO. They do prevent Story 1.2 from closing and must be addressed through architecture/correct-course before a new, separately approved live run.
